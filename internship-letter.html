To whom it may concern,

MedTrack is a project-based healthcare and clinical practice management backend. The internship is structured around real software-engineering workflows rather than tutorial-only exercises. Interns work from defined feature tickets, translate business requirements into backend designs, implement maintainable code, write automated tests, and participate in Git-based review and delivery practices.
